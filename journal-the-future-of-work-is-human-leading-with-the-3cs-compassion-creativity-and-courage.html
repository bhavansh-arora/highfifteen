The future of work is human: leading with compassion, creativity and courage

Vanessa BelleauFounder, executive coach & speaker
Share

There is a quiet revolution happening in boardrooms, Zoom rooms and team meetings around the world.

Yes, you guessed it, the old blueprint of leadership - built on control, competition and compliance is being thrown in the bin!

It has been proven time and time again that it does not guarantee long-term high performance for any companies. Trust us when we say this. We have worked with enough companies to know that leadership needs a new definition. It needs to be much more compassionate, creative and courageous.

Leadership that wins is the one that sees people not as ‘resources’ but as humans. With inner worlds. With potential. With boundaries. With brilliance.

This is not softness. This is strategy. Yet, it is also important for us to mention that there is nothing wrong with a bit of softness.

We truly believe that organisations that centre emotional intelligence, strategic foresight, agility and inclusion are not just doing good - they are doing well. Some of our clients believe this and we see it, they see it and most importantly their employees see it and do their best to deliver the mission.

So, why the 3Cs? And why now?

In a world where change is constant, pressure is high and let’s be honest, people are over many things…
The 3Cs can be your competitive edge for your workplace. They help you to retain talent and

Let’s break it down:

1. Compassion: the new foundation of high trust teams

The best leaders today don’t just know how to lead meetings — they know how to hold space.

Compassion isn’t about being passive. It’s about presence.

It’s the ability to:

✔️ Recognise what your team is going through (without fixing or rescuing)
✔️ Offer a level of empathy and accountability
✔️ Create psychological safety where honesty and vulnerability can exist

Leaders who lead with compassion see higher engagement, loyalty and innovation.
Why? Because people don’t shut down or shrink back — they show up.

2. Creativity: the core skill for agility and innovation

Creativity isn’t just for the design team, unlike popular belief!
It is a leadership skill and it is essential for navigating the unknown in a way that promotes progress and success.

Creative leaders:

✔️ See possibilities where others see problems
✔️ Give permission to experiment, fail and iterate
✔️ Inspire fresh thinking by modelling openness and curiosity

And here is the most important point: inclusion fuels creativity.
When you bring diverse voices into the room, and actually listen to them, you get richer ideas, faster problem-solving and more relevant solutions.

At High Fifteen, we support leaders to connect with their own creativity and design inclusive systems where creativity can affect the whole team.

3. Courage: The catalyst for culture change

Let’s be honest: courageous leadership is uncomfortable.

Because it means:

✔️ Speaking up when it’s easier to stay quiet
✔️ Making values-based decisions, even when they are unpopular
✔️ Owning your mistakes and learning in public
✔️ Letting go of performative leadership and embracing reality and realness

Yet, courage is what makes transformation stick, especially when it comes to inclusion, equity and high performance.

Our take on the 3C leadership

We work and coach leaders, CEOs and execs who are ready to stop ticking boxes and start leading with backbone and heart.

Here is what we are seeing:

🟢 Top talent is choosing values-aligned organisations
🟢 Employees are demanding more from their leaders than ever before: more empathy, more transparency and more humanity
 🟢 The most resilient companies are investing in culture AND systems

This isn’t just a trend. It’s a shift.
And those who lead it will thrive.
