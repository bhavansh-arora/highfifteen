Straight talk

Are we right for each other?

We do our best work with people who are ready for honest challenge. Here is how to tell.

We’re a great fit if…

  • You lead, or support, senior leaders and teams where performance really matters
  • Something important is at risk: delivery, retention, trust or reputation
  • You want direct, warm challenge, not flattery or theory
  • You are ready to act on what we uncover together
  • You see inclusion and performance as the same conversation

We’re probably not for you if…

  • You want an off-the-shelf session to tick a box
  • You expect us to ‘fix’ people without leadership involvement
  • You are looking for quick wins with no behaviour change
  • There is no time or appetite to work between sessions
