How are coaching sessions delivered?

Sessions usually run virtually via Zoom or Google Meet, so they are accessible wherever you are. In-person sessions can be arranged on request.

How long does a programme last?

A coaching programme lasts a minimum of three months, giving you space to build deep self-awareness, refine your approach and create lasting change. The longer you work with your coach, the more significant the shift in behaviour and action.

What is the difference between coaching, mentoring and consulting?
  • A mentor shares personal career experience and advice
  • A consultant provides expert solutions and strategies
  • A coach helps you unlock your own insights, refine your leadership approach and take action towards your goals
Will I get support between sessions?

Yes. You will have ongoing support through check-ins and direct messaging via WhatsApp or Telegram, so you are never alone in your leadership journey.

How do I know if coaching is right for me?

It is the right fit if you are:

  • An ambitious professional ready to elevate your impact
  • Facing leadership challenges and need a trusted thought partner
  • Committed to personal and professional growth
  • Willing to be challenged and take action
