Our story

Turning challenges into opportunities, silos into synergy.

In 2019, amid shifting workplace paradigms and growing complexity, Vanessa Belleau, a seasoned brand strategist, executive coach and former Consultancy EMEA Director at WGSN, set out to redefine how organisations align culture with strategy and creativity.

Her mission was clear: help leaders and teams focus on what matters by turning bold ideas into measurable results.

Today we help leaders and teams perform well in complex conditions without burnout, blame or breakdown, by strengthening decision-making, leadership confidence and the quality of the everyday conversations that drive results.

Meet our founder

Vanessa Belleau

Founder, executive coach, speaker (she/her)

Vanessa’s career spans two decades at the intersection of strategic business insight, executive coaching and cultural transformation. She combines sharp strategic thinking with a deep understanding of human behaviour to inspire meaningful change.

Her work spans corporates, scale-ups, charities and creative organisations, where she has helped leaders stabilise performance, rebuild trust and lead with clarity in demanding environments. Clients value her calm authority, direct style and ability to work with complexity without over-simplifying it.

Vanessa is known for her ability to
