Short bio

Vanessa Belleau is the founder of High Fifteen Ltd, a culture and inclusion consultancy that has impacted over 30,000 employees and leaders through workshops, culture strategy and executive coaching. With 20+ years of experience and a client list spanning global brands such as LVMH, Levi’s, Nestlé, CBRE, Nike, Sony Music and Disney, as well as charities and scale-ups, Vanessa helps teams unlock high performance by putting people and purpose at the heart of business without compromising the bottom line.

A trusted advisor to executive teams and boards, she is also a TEDx speaker and the creative behind Beautiful Water Studio, where she paints vibrant, story-rich pieces exploring identity, resilience and joy, with her dog Purple as head critic.
