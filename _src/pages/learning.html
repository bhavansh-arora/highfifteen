FAQs

Before you book.

How long do workshops or programmes run?

Workshops run anywhere from 60 minutes to four hours. Programmes are designed for ongoing development, and many organisations run them over several months for sustained impact.

Can workshops be customised for our organisation?

Yes. We work closely with HR and senior stakeholders to align content with your strategy and address your specific challenges and goals.

How do you measure success?

We collect feedback, analyse engagement data and assess confidence levels after training. Past participants rated usefulness of training 5/5 and trainer expertise 5/5.

How are workshops delivered?

In person and online, so they are accessible to every employee regardless of location or learning preference.

What makes the programme inclusive?

Equal access to learning at every level. Sessions are tailored by level but delivered cross-functionally, which builds networks, collaboration and knowledge-sharing.
