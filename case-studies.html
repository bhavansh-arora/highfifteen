Team cultural transformation

From ‘storming’ to ‘performing’ in eight months.

Using a blend of executive and group coaching and facilitation tools, we guided 15 highly skilled, passionate individuals to thrive while creating an inclusively safe team environment. The team’s sentiment about its own culture went from 75% negative mentions to less than 10%.

Empowering women

One keynote. A standing ovation. Two years of summits.

120 senior womenKeynote → workshops → emceeLevi’s HQ, Belgium
