Team cultural transformation

From ‘storming’ to ‘performing’ in eight months.

A new, fast-growing team with a lot of responsibility needed to define its role, its culture and what success would look like. Eight months later it was consistently performing, and an essential part of the wider business.

The brief

What was at stake.

We supported the cultural development of multiple Sony Music teams through workshops and coaching.

One team came to us at a difficult time. In the words of its director: “We were a new team in the business, were growing quickly and had a lot of responsibility. We needed to understand our role as a team and help define our team culture and what our future success would look like.”

What we did

How we got there.

Understand the whole system

Time taken to fully understand the business, the team, its needs, challenges and strengths, and the many, sometimes competing, dynamics.

Executive + group coaching

A blend of executive and group coaching, delivered over several sessions.

Facilitation that feels safe

Facilitation tools that established a safe and open environment for the team to express themselves.

A path to success

The tools to address the team’s challenges and set a path to success.

The impact

What changed.

The team progressed from ‘storming’ to consistently ‘performing’ in just eight months, while building a positive, thriving and compassionate team environment.

Its overall sentiment about the team culture went from 75% negative mentions to less than 10%, and it developed into a high-performing team and an essential part of the wider business.
